How the typing test works

Choose a mode, duration and difficulty, click the text and start typing. The countdown begins on your first keystroke, and stops the moment time runs out or you finish the passage. Correct characters turn white, mistakes turn red, and Backspace lets you fix them.

Scoring: every five characters (spaces included) count as one word. Your WPM is the net speed: (characters typed ÷ 5 − uncorrected errors) ÷ minutes. Accuracy is correct characters divided by characters typed. Results are a measure of this test only, not a ranking.

    Practice mode and typing tips

    Practice mode has no countdown, so you can focus on rhythm and accuracy. Finish the passage or press End practice when you are done.

    Typing tips

    • Posture: sit upright, keep your feet flat, elbows near 90 degrees and wrists relaxed rather than resting hard on the desk.
    • Finger placement: rest your fingers on the home row (A S D F and J K L ;) with thumbs on the space bar, and return to it after each key.
    • Accuracy: slow down until you make few mistakes. Speed follows accuracy, not the other way round.
    • Eyes: look at the screen, not the keyboard. Short, regular sessions beat one long one.

    What is a good typing speed?

    It depends on your goal. Many people type around 40 words per minute. Around 50 to 60 WPM is comfortable for most office work, 70 to 80 WPM is fast, and anything above that is exceptional. For programmers and writers, steady accuracy often matters more than top speed.

    How is WPM calculated?

    WPM stands for words per minute. Because words differ in length, typing tests use a standard word of five characters, including spaces. Gross WPM is characters typed divided by five, divided by minutes. Net WPM subtracts uncorrected errors, which is the figure TypeRush shows.

    How can you improve typing speed?

    Practise a little every day, use all ten fingers, keep your eyes off the keys and build a steady rhythm. Review the words you get wrong, and only push for speed once your accuracy is consistently high.

    Why is typing accuracy important?

    Every mistake needs a Backspace and a retype, which costs more time than typing carefully. High accuracy also makes emails, code and documents cleaner, and builds the muscle memory that lets your speed grow naturally.
